## 摘要
统一多模态模型（UMMs）在共享参数上同时训练图像理解与自回归图像生成，而这两个目标已知会相互干扰。现有的诊断与补救方法在层级或专家（expert）粒度上运作，逐层度量冲突并通过分离参数来化解。我们认为，这种粒度掩盖了一个正交的维度。UMM 中的生成是对视觉 token 光栅序列的下一 token 预测，而序列中 token 的角色随位置系统性地变化，因此生成梯度对理解的干扰强度应取决于它源自序列中的何处。我们引入位置解析的干扰图，将理解—生成梯度冲突归因到每一层内视觉 token 的位置上，其计算只需一次反向传播，成本为标准反向传播的 1.2 倍。在 Show-o 与 Janus-Pro 上，在控制深度后，位置解释了冲突方差中很大的一部分（偏 η²=0.31，而层在 Show-o 上为 0.35；在 Janus-Pro 上为 0.15 对 0.30）：序列前四分之一的梯度与理解的平均余弦为 −0.18，后四分之一为 −0.02。这种依赖关系在经过逐位置梯度范数归一化后依然存在，保留了 80% 的效应量，且冲突强度与语义内容相关（Spearman ρ=0.64）。基于该图，我们提出位置感知调制（PAM），它仅在高冲突位置移除生成梯度中反对齐的分量，而不改变架构。在匹配可训练参数预算下，PAM 在 Show-o 上相较逐层分离提升了 +21 MME 和 +2.4 GenEval 点，同时在 POPE 和整体 FID 上与之相当；随机位置对照仅恢复了约 31% 的增益。基于位置与基于层级的分离是互补的自由度，可以结合使用。

## Abstract
Unified multimodal models (UMMs) train image understanding and autoregressive image generation on shared parameters, and the two objectives are known to interfere. Existing diagnoses and remedies operate at the resolution of layers or experts, measuring conflict per layer and resolving it by separating parameters. We argue that this resolution hides an orthogonal axis. Generation in a UMM is next-token prediction over a raster sequence of visual tokens whose roles vary systematically with position, so how strongly a generation gradient interferes with understanding should depend on where in the sequence it originates. We introduce a position-resolved interference map that attributes understanding-generation gradient conflict to visual-token positions within every layer, computed from a single backward pass at $1.2\times$ the cost of a standard backward pass. On Show-o and Janus-Pro, position explains a large share of conflict variance after controlling for depth (partial $η^2=0.31$ vs. $0.35$ for layer on Show-o; $0.15$ vs. $0.30$ on Janus-Pro): the first quarter of the sequence has a mean gradient cosine of $-0.18$ against understanding, the last quarter $-0.02$. The dependence survives per-position gradient-norm normalization, retaining $80%$ of its effect size, and conflict strength tracks semantic content (Spearman $ρ=0.64$). Building on the map, we propose position-aware modulation (PAM), which removes the anti-aligned component of generation gradients only at high-conflict positions without changing the architecture. Under a matched trainable-parameter budget, PAM improves over layer-wise separation by $+21$ MME and $+2.4$ GenEval points on Show-o while matching it on POPE and overall FID; a random-position control recovers about $31%$ of the gain. Position-based and layer-based separation are complementary degrees of freedom and can be combined.
