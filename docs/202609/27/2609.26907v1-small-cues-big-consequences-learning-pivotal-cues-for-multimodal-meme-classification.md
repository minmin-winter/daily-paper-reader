## 摘要
模因往往从其微小但具有决定性的视觉、文本或跨模态线索中获得有害、仇恨或讽刺的含义。现有的多模态分类器在主要依赖全局图像-文本表示时，可能会漏掉此类证据。我们提出了 MemeCF，一个聚焦线索的基准，包含 9,895 个模因，涵盖伤害、仇恨和讽刺，并带有标注，用于识别关键证据的模态和依据。我们还提出了 MemePIVOT，一种用于模因分类的局部-全局架构。MemePIVOT 使用冻结的 CLIP 特征、非平衡最优传输来将单词与图像块对齐，同时允许无关证据保持未匹配，并使用证据融合头在不确定性下将局部定位与全局模因上下文相结合。在 HarMeme、PrideMM 和 MemeCF 上的实验表明，其相较于强大的仅文本、仅图像、多模态和视觉-语言基线均取得一致提升。跨数据集和消融结果进一步表明，显式的关键证据建模提高了鲁棒性，并在全局多模态表示之外做出有意义的贡献。我们的代码和数据集公开于 https://github.com/AkshitSharma1/MemePIVOT

## Abstract
Memes often derive their harmful, hateful, or sarcastic meaning from small but decisive visual, textual, or cross-modal cues. Existing multimodal classifiers can miss such evidence when relying mainly on global image-text representations. We introduce MemeCF, a cue-focused benchmark of 9,895 memes across harm, hate, and sarcasm, with annotations identifying the modality and rationale of the pivotal evidence. We also propose MemePIVOT, a local-global architecture for meme classification. MemePIVOT uses frozen CLIP features, unbalanced optimal transport to align words with image patches while allowing irrelevant evidence to remain unmatched, and an evidential fusion head to combine local grounding with global meme context under uncertainty. Experiments on HarMeme, PrideMM, and MemeCF show consistent gains over strong text-only, image-only, multimodal, and vision-language baselines. Cross-dataset and ablation results further show that explicit pivotal-evidence modeling improves robustness and contributes meaningfully beyond global multimodal representations. Our code and dataset are publicly available at https://github.com/AkshitSharma1/MemePIVOT
